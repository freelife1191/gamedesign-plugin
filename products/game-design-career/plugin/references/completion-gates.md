# Career completion gates

Record each applicable gate as `pending`, `blocked`, `approved`, or `not-applicable`. Missing evidence does not pass a gate.

## Route and evidence

- Record the stage, intent, target, assumptions, selected skills, selected roles, desired output, and next action.
- Keep selected roles at three or fewer and preserve identical roles and questions across parallel and sequential review modes.
- Cite each time-sensitive claim with fresh primary evidence, its source URL, posted date when available, and retrieval date. Mark unavailable verification as an evidence gap.
- Tie each candidate claim to an inspectable artifact, project record, source, feedback record, or explicit verification task.

## Responsible career guidance

- Confirm that no hiring outcome is promised and no path is presented as universally correct.
- Do not infer suitability from age, education, major, or employment gap. Compare observable evidence, constraints, interests, and role requirements instead.
- Confirm that no experience, metric, or interview story is fabricated. Keep unknowns explicit and define a verification or evidence-building action.
- For third-party game data, text, screenshots, or images, record the source, use purpose, and rights or quotation note before portfolio handoff.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty as decision records. Do not average away a blocking finding or silently accept residual risk.

## Handoff

- Verify that required artifact sections, evidence gaps, review findings, and minimum repairs are present.
- Confirm that the canonical artifact is preserved when an optional reviewer, visualization tool, renderer, or export format is unavailable. Record a structured warning and resumable next step.
- Approve handoff only when all applicable blockers are cleared by evidence or assigned to a named human decision owner.
