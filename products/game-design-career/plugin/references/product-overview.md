# Game Design Career

Game Design Career turns career questions into evidence-backed, reviewable artifacts. It supports four stages: entry, new hire, junior growth, and transition. Routing considers both the user's stage and intent, then selects one primary skill and no more than three bounded review roles.

The workflow does not promise hiring outcomes or declare one universally correct career. When the stage or target role is unclear, it produces a game-design role map with multiple provisional paths, tradeoffs, evidence gaps, and next experiments.

## Evidence policy

Current job postings, employers, projects, regions, employment types, compensation, laws, engine or tool preferences, hiring volume, and market outlook are time-sensitive. Claims about them require fresh, dated primary evidence. Outputs distinguish cited facts from inference and record uncertainty, sample limits, region, and retrieval date. Missing evidence becomes an explicit research or verification task.

Candidate experience, ownership, team size, metrics, and results are never fabricated. Portfolio use of third-party game data or images records the source, purpose, and rights or quotation basis.

## Routing contract

`routing.json` is the deterministic routing source. Every route declares its career stage, intent, required evidence, primary skill, bounded roles, current-research trigger, artifact type, and completion gates. Later skill and role packages implement the IDs reserved by that contract.
