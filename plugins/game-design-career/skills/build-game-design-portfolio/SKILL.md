# Build Game Design Portfolio

## Overview

Design the portfolio as an evidence trail, not a gallery. Make the reader able to locate what happened, distinguish the author's contribution, and judge the decision without relying on unsupported narration.

## Load Reference

Read `../../references/methods/portfolio-evidence.md` before drafting or revising a case study. Use its exact sequence, claim record, recovery contract, and completion gate.

## Build the Case

1. Name the target competency and intended reviewer. Choose at least one capability to expose: design judgment, implementation handoff, data reasoning, playtest learning, scope control, collaboration, or iteration.
2. Inventory every material claim before writing prose. Give each claim a stable `claimId`, evidence address and provenance, strength and status, recovery owner and action, and `inspectabilityGate`.
3. Follow the reference sequence exactly. Preserve facts, participant statements, interpretations, and unsupported claims as different statuses.
4. Attribute team work and personal work separately. Do not invent ownership, metrics, team size, implementation, test results, or business impact.
5. For missing evidence, keep the claim in the same record and assign a concrete recovery action. Replace unverifiable certainty with a bounded statement; do not fill the artifact with context-free placeholders.
6. Use diagrams, screenshots, and visual hierarchy only when they reveal relationships, state, sequence, or comparison. Visual polish must not substitute for inspectable competence evidence.
7. End each decision with alternatives considered, the chosen constraint, result or current status, and what would change the next decision.

## Output Contract

Return the ordered case study, a claim-to-evidence index, attribution and rights notes, recovery queue, and reviewer inspection checklist. Evidence addresses must point to an artifact section, source URL, file/version, test record, annotated image, or explicitly unavailable source.

## Completion

Finish only when every material claim has a complete evidence record and the reviewer can locate, interpret, and verify the selected competency without asking the author where the proof is. If an essential claim fails its `inspectabilityGate`, keep the case study incomplete and report the assigned recovery owner and action.
